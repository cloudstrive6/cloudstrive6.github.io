Privacy Policy

TFS Automation · Last updated 26 September 2026

TFS Automation and TFS Publisher ("the apps") are internal tools operated by The Filipino Standard to publish content to, and read analytics for, The Filipino Standard's own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account. They are used only by The Filipino Standard's owner and are not offered to other users.

What the app accesses

When the channel owner authorizes the app with Google, it may use these YouTube permissions:

  • youtube.upload: to upload and schedule The Filipino Standard's own videos, titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails.
  • youtube.readonly: to read the channel's own videos and their metadata.
  • yt-analytics.readonly: to read aggregate performance reports for the channel's own videos (views, watch time, retention, click-through rate, subscribers gained).

The app also uses the public YouTube Data API to read publicly available trending-video information.

On Meta platforms, TFS Publisher uses the owner's own Facebook Page and Instagram professional account permissions (pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_posts, publish_video, instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish, instagram_manage_insights, read_insights, business_management) only to publish The Filipino Standard's posts, Reels and carousels and to read their aggregate performance.

How data is used

Data is used only to publish The Filipino Standard's content and to understand which of our videos perform well so we can improve future ones. We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone, do not use it for advertising, and do not use it to train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored as encrypted secrets in our private deployment environment. Analytics reports are stored in a private database used only by the app. Aggregate analytics are kept for up to 24 months and then deleted.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API use is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke the app's access at any time from Google Account → Security → Third-party access. Revoking access stops all uploads and analytics reads; stored analytics are then deleted within 30 days. For Facebook and Instagram, remove TFS Publisher under Facebook Settings → Business integrations. See also our data deletion instructions.

Contact

Questions about this policy: message The Filipino Standard on Facebook or via the YouTube channel's About page.
