Data Deletion Instructions

TFS Automation & TFS Publisher · Last updated 26 September 2026

Our apps store no personal data about anyone other than The Filipino Standard's own accounts. They only keep access tokens for our own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account, plus aggregate performance numbers for our own posts.

To remove the apps' access and delete their data

  1. Facebook / Instagram: Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations → select TFS Publisher → Remove. For Instagram, Settings → Apps and websites → Remove.
  2. YouTube / Google: Google Account → Third-party access → TFS Automation → Remove access.

Once access is removed, our stored tokens stop working and are deleted, along with any stored analytics, within 30 days. To request deletion directly, message The Filipino Standard on Facebook.
